> Historical mechanism Source: this document remains controlling for formal design-resource authoring/preflight and Long-Task selected-design Fact/Obligation closure. [`workflow-assurance-boundary.md`](workflow-assurance-boundary.md) supersedes only the clauses that previously required the default Workflow Contract to reproduce exact per-Fact production-conformance accounting; shared design Source authority and every Long-Task rule below remain unchanged.

<!-- ty-source-item:start key=design-fact-completeness-result kind=outcome_result -->
Formal selected Web/App implementation resources default to complete observable-design-fact granularity: authoring derives the full material in-scope Expected Fact Universe, canonical resources express and independently inventory it, and the residual handoff indexes the same exact facts. Default work consumes affected targets and conditions through their production owners and evidence-bounded current-candidate checks; Long-Task additionally preserves every Fact through exact per-Fact current-snapshot results. When an exact target carries a complete platform-specific pixel reference, pixel fidelity remains a material Source requirement rather than an optional preference.
<!-- ty-source-item:end -->

<!-- ty-source-item:start key=default-observable-design-fact-granularity kind=requirement -->
UI/UX design-resource consumption must default to observable design facts rather than semantic Control granularity: the inventory includes every material layout geometry, pixel appearance, design token, visible content, component or small-element anatomy and style, component state, interaction, motion, responsive behavior, input behavior, accessibility semantic, and asset fact that the selected resources explicitly express for an in-scope target and condition.
<!-- ty-source-item:end -->

<!-- ty-source-item:start key=resource-fact-inventory-closure kind=requirement -->
The selected handoff must contain one exhaustive, stable-keyed design-fact inventory plus an explicit closure disposition for every selected target resource; every fact binds one subject, target, atomic condition, UI/UX dimension, typed resource evidence, originating Source Item, and required verification method, while unreadable, unsupported, conflicting, unclassified, unresolved, or unavailable material meaning blocks readiness.
<!-- ty-source-item:end -->

<!-- ty-source-item:start key=pixel-fidelity-by-expression kind=requirement -->
Every selected `exact_target` must carry full-target layout-geometry and visual-pixel facts for every declared atomic condition, and the validator must derive `layout_geometry` and `visual_pixel` as required methods from those facts; a target without sufficient immutable information for that comparison must remain a constraint, unresolved, or unavailable instead of silently weakening to approximate fidelity.
<!-- ty-source-item:end -->

<!-- ty-source-item:start key=complete-fact-consumption kind=requirement -->
Both routes must map selected design meaning to its production owner and real root-entry journey, use current-candidate observations, and avoid claiming conditions that were not established. Long-Task alone must conserve the exact selected Fact set without sampling or silent grouping, carry every method and condition through exact Fact references and independently attributable current-execution results, and block machine acceptance for any missing, duplicate, mismatched, unimplemented, unexecuted, stale, failed or indistinguishable Fact. Default work reports the corresponding unchecked or unresolved scope without rebuilding that exact result set.
<!-- ty-source-item:end -->

<!-- ty-source-item:start key=honest-expression-boundary kind=requirement -->
The guarantee remains bounded by what selected readable resources explicitly express: a static image governs all shown pixels for its declared condition but does not invent unshown interaction, motion, responsive, input, permission, recovery, or accessibility behavior; unsupported formats or facts remain blocking, and the named project design extractor or oracle remains an explicit trusted-computing-boundary component rather than an assumed universal semantic interpreter.
<!-- ty-source-item:end -->

<!-- ty-source-item:start key=non-ui-and-control-semantics-preserved kind=requirement -->
Non-UI requirements continue to close as atomic Requirements, Technical Obligations, Constraints, Forbidden Shortcuts, Claims, and Assertions exactly as expressed, while the existing 22-field Control and cross-Control relation model remains the semantic interaction owner and is not repurposed into a registry of every image, element, style property, or implementation `div`.
<!-- ty-source-item:end -->

<!-- ty-source-item:start key=public-guidance-and-authoring-default kind=requirement -->
Package-managed authoring, UI/UX, engineering, Long-Task, default-workflow, English, Chinese, and package README guidance must all teach the same Source boundary: inspect affected selected resources and conditions, preserve explicit limitations, treat expressed pixel fidelity as material, and never describe handoff preflight or resource integrity as production conformance. Formal authoring/preflight and Long-Task retain complete Fact/resource closure; default production work uses attributable project checks and reports what they did not establish.
<!-- ty-source-item:end -->

<!-- ty-source-item:start key=formal-authoring-design-obligation-universe kind=requirement -->
For a formal selected Web/App implementation handoff, `design-resource-authoring` must default to deriving the complete material in-scope UI/UX decision universe before commissioning and must iterate the provider until every obligation is expressed by readable canonical implementation resources or has an explicit `not_applicable`, `excluded_by_scope`, `decision_required`, or `unavailable` disposition. It is not sufficient to inventory only what an initially generated page, default state, screenshot, broad dimension row, or provider summary happened to contain; the authoring obligation starts from the requested surfaces, product semantics, adopted design system, target platforms, applicable observable-condition axes, subject hierarchy, and atomic property catalog. Its atomic identity is one applicable `subject × target × condition × variation × property` Fact Cell.
<!-- ty-source-item:end -->

<!-- ty-source-item:start key=independent-canonical-fact-manifest kind=requirement -->

<!-- ty-source-item:end -->

<!-- ty-source-item:start key=subject-hierarchy-instance-anatomy-closure kind=requirement -->

<!-- ty-source-item:start key=compound-applicability-and-precedence-closure kind=requirement -->

<!-- ty-source-item:start key=platform-localization-system-and-render-target-closure kind=requirement -->
